About Sarah

A lifelong dream, sung from southern Minnesota.

Sarah Holdyn has been writing songs for as long as she can remember. Raised in Austin, Minnesota, she was performing her own originals before she hit her teens. For more than a decade, she’s been a regular voice on the National Anthem at events across southeast Minnesota. She’s led worship for years too. It’s where she learned that a song only lands when you mean every word.

After stepping away from music for almost a year following the loss of her dad, she came back in 2024 knowing one thing: this is the dream, and she’s not done chasing it.

Today she plays fairs, festivals and private events across the Upper Midwest with a full band, or stripped down for smaller rooms. Her set runs from Dolly Parton and Jo Dee Messina to Carrie Underwood and Taylor Swift, with originals woven in. It pulls you in, keeps you smiling between songs and sends you home humming.
