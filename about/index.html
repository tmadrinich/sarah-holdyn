About Sarah

A songwriter since childhood. A performer for over a decade.

Sarah is a singer, songwriter and musician based in the Upper Midwest. What started as a childhood pastime has grown into a music career — original songs built on live instruments and a passionate voice that tells stories.

Alongside her originals, Sarah and the band play the country and country-pop songs your crowd already knows by heart.

  • Rascal Flatts
  • Taylor Swift
  • Keith Urban
  • Maddie & Tae
  • Blake Shelton
  • Carrie Underwood
  • Tenille Arts
